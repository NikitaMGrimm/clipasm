# AI contribution policy

AI tools and coding agents are welcome as assistants in ClipAsm contributions.
They do not replace human judgment or accountability.

A human contributor is responsible for every submitted change. The contributor
must review and understand the work, be able to explain it, and ensure that it
meets the same testing, documentation, security, and quality requirements as
any other contribution.

Disclose material AI assistance in the pull request or contribution note.
Trivial autocomplete does not need disclosure.

AI tools may work autonomously in a contributor-controlled checkout or branch,
but public submission remains a human action. An agent must not push to a shared
remote, open a pull request, file an issue, or communicate with maintainers
without explicit human review and approval. Maintainer-facing text drafted with
AI must also be reviewed and revised as needed so that it accurately represents
the human contributor's understanding and intent.

This policy governs human contributions made with AI assistance. Repository
agent instructions, such as [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md), separately govern how an
agent operates in the repository.
